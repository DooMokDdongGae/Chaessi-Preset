# Help artwork and screenshots

Artwork in these guides was supplied by the project owner for public help documentation. The original artwork remains with its owner. Screenshots show the CHAESSI PRESET interface.

The workflow montage and earlier image-tool examples are from v3.8.1. They do not show results from the v3.8.2 absolute camera. Captions distinguish these examples from current interface captures and from input or mask preparation.

The maintained online guide uses the image inventory in [web/help/images.json](../../web/help/images.json). Versioned inventories describe the images included in their original releases. Source licenses and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) remain applicable.
